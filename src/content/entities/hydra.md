Founded as a research arm and organised around the belief that power should be
concentrated rather than shared. It builds better weapons than the state that
funds it, and by the time anyone notices, it no longer meaningfully answers to
that state.

Worth following as an entity precisely because it recurs in places where nobody
expects it. An organisation is not a thing that appears in a scene the way an
object does, but it is exactly the kind of thread this catalogue exists to trace.
