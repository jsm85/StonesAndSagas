An infantry weapon from the invading force, functional, portable, and — after the
battle — simply lying around.

A deliberately small entity, included to show that the model is not only for
artefacts of cosmic significance. The interesting cross-reference is often the
mundane object with consequences, and this one is a whole short film's plot.
