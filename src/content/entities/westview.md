A commuter town with a high street, a neighbourhood watch and a talent show. What
makes it worth an entry is that its ordinariness is a construction, maintained
at considerable cost by someone who needs it to hold.

Included as a location entity because places are followed across the catalogue in
the same way objects are — and because a town that appears across several
episodes, rather than in one film, is exactly the case that episodes-as-timeline-units
exists to handle.
