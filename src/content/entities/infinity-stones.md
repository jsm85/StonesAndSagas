Six objects predating the universe as it currently exists, each concentrating a
single fundamental force — space, mind, reality, power, time, soul. Individually
each is more than most beings can survive using. Together they are decisive.

They function in this catalogue as the connective tissue: the thread that runs
through the greatest number of titles, and the clearest case for cross-references
existing at all. Each stone is its own entry, grouped under this one.
