A cube of unusual stability containing the Space Stone. It is not itself an
Infinity Stone, a distinction almost nobody on screen makes, and the confusion is
load-bearing: people keep treating it as a battery when it is a door.

Kept as its own entry rather than folded into the Space Stone because the two are
followed separately. Titles that show the cube and titles that show the stone are
not the same list, and merging them would lose the point at which one becomes the
other.
