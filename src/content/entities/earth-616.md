The designation for the primary reality of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, used on
screen rather than invented here.

It matters to this site as a sorting concern rather than a plot one. A single
in-universe ordering only works while everything shares one chronology; once
material set in other realities enters the catalogue, positions have to say which
line they belong to or the sequence becomes nonsense. Every timeline entry
without an explicit reality is assumed to be this one.
