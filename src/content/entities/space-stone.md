The stone concerned with position: what is next to what, and how far anything
truly is from anything else. Used deliberately it makes distance optional.

For most of its screen life it is not visible at all, sitting inside a container
that is easier to hold and easier to misunderstand. That gap — between the object
people think they have and the thing actually inside it — is why the stone and
its container are separate entries here.
