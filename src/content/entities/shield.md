A response agency, founded to handle what conventional institutions are not
equipped for and to keep it out of public view. Its work is mostly containment
and cataloguing, which is unglamorous and constant.

It sits opposite HYDRA in the catalogue in more ways than one, and the
relationship between the two is the sort of thing a cross-reference view should
make legible rather than leaving to whoever has watched enough to notice.
