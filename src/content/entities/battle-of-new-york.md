An incursion through a portal over Manhattan, ended in a few hours and referred
back to for years afterwards.

It earns its own entry because so much later material is positioned relative to
it rather than to a date: things happen before it, or in the years after it, and
characters describe themselves in terms of where they were when it happened. That
is what an event entity is for — it carries a position in the chronology
independent of any single title, which is why this one has a `timeline` block of
its own.
