Steve Rogers is refused by the army, volunteers for an experimental programme,
and comes out of it transformed. The scientist responsible is shot before the
process can be repeated.

The character was created by Joe Simon and Jack Kirby at Timely Comics, the
company that later became Marvel, and arrived as an explicitly political
proposition at a moment when that was contested rather than obvious. The film
inherits the plot; this is where the argument came from.
