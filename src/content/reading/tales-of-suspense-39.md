The issue that introduces Iron Man, in an anthology title that had until then
been running short science-fiction and suspense stories. Stark is injured and
captured while observing a war, is told he has days to live, and builds a chest
device to keep himself alive and a suit around it to get himself out.

Worth reading before or after the film for how little needed changing. The
armour is grey and enormous, the politics belong firmly to 1963, and the shape
of the story underneath is the one the MCU opened with forty-five years later.
