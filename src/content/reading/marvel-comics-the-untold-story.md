A narrative history of Marvel from the 1930s onward, drawing on interviews with
people who worked there. It is largely about labour and credit: who created
what, who was paid for it, and how the company's account of its own past was
assembled.

Listed here as background for the comics above rather than for any single film.
It is a prose book, and it is included partly to prove the point that the
recommended-reading model is not comics-only.
