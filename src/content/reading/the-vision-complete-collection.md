Vision moves to the suburbs, builds a family, and sets out to be unremarkable.
The series follows what happens as the household's attempts to pass for ordinary
accumulate consequences none of them will admit to.

It is narrated at a deliberate distance, in the register of a tragedy that has
already happened, and it is the strongest argument in print for reading Vision
as a character rather than a device. Recommended alongside WandaVision for the
same reason: both take the suburb seriously as a place where something is being
performed.
