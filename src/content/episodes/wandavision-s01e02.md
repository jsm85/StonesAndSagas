The decade moves on and the episode moves with it, into the late 1960s. Wanda
and Vision rehearse a stage-magic routine for a neighbourhood talent show, which
requires them both to hide what they can actually do in plain view of an
audience.

An object comes into the town from outside, in colour, and is dealt with rather
than acknowledged. The episode ends by rewinding a scene it has already played,
which is the first unambiguous evidence that someone is directing this — and the
reason these two episodes sit at separate positions on the timeline rather than
