Tony Stark demonstrates a new missile system for the US military, is ambushed on
the way back, and wakes up in a cave with a car battery wired into his chest and
his own ordnance stacked against the wall. He builds a suit to walk out.

Home again, he announces that Stark Industries is leaving the weapons business —
a decision the company's board, and the man who has been running it in his
absence, treat as a problem to be managed. The rest of the film is Stark
refining the suit while working out who sold him to his captors, and it sets the
pattern the series runs on for a decade: the technology is the easy part, and
the person operating it is not.
