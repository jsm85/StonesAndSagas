Steve Rogers wants to enlist and keeps being told no, until a scientist running a
super-soldier programme decides that the interesting question is not who is
strongest but who has been small long enough to have learned something from it.
The procedure works. The programme is then shut down by an assassin's bullet,
leaving one soldier and no way to make another.

What he ends up fighting is not the regular war. HYDRA, the science division
under Johann Schmidt, has found an artefact of enormous power and is building
weapons around it, and the film's back half is a campaign against that rather
than a conventional advance. It ends with a deliberate crash into ice, which is
how a 1940s character arrives in the present without ageing — the earliest
position on this site's timeline, and one of the last films in release order to
reach it.
