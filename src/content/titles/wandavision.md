Each episode adopts a different era of American situation comedy — the aspect
ratio, the film stock, the laugh track — and each one develops a crack that the
form cannot contain. The question of what is producing the town and why is the
season's spine.

Structurally this is the reason the catalogue models episodes rather than
series. WandaVision's nine episodes do not occupy a single point in the
chronology alongside the films around them; they progress, and the events inside
them are separated by hours and days that the timeline has to be able to
express. The series entry holds what is true of the whole run — its creator, its
certificate, its principal cast — and the episodes carry everything that has a
position in time.
