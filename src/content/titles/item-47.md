New York has been invaded and cleaned up, and a quantity of alien hardware is
unaccounted for. Two people out of work and out of money find a functioning
Chitauri rifle and start robbing banks with it, which puts them on a S.H.I.E.L.D.
recovery list rather than an ordinary police one.

The short runs twelve minutes and exists to answer a question the film it
attaches to skips: what happens to a city afterwards, and to the people who pick
things up off the street. It is a small piece of the catalogue that carries real
cross-references — the Chitauri weapons, the recovery operation, and Sitwell's
place inside S.H.I.E.L.D. before that becomes complicated.
