---
name: Peggy Carter
summary: An SSR officer who saw what Rogers was before the serum did, and outlasted the war he vanished from.
---

Peggy Carter is an intelligence officer in an organisation that keeps
underestimating her, and she is right about Steve Rogers before anyone with
authority is. Her competence is treated as unremarkable by the story and
remarkable by everyone in it, which is roughly the point.
