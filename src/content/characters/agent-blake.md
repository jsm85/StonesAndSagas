---
name: Agent Blake
summary: The senior S.H.I.E.L.D. agent running the recovery of missing Chitauri hardware.
---

Blake supervises the sweep for weapons left behind after the Battle of New York,
and treats the assignment with the impatience of someone who expected a more
interesting war. He is a useful measure of how S.H.I.E.L.D. talks about clean-up
work when no one senior is listening.
