An engineer of real ability and a founder with an instinct for where the money
was, Howard Stark spends the war building for the government and the decades
afterwards building a company around what he learned doing it.

He is a useful test of how this catalogue models a character. He is cast in some
titles and merely invoked in others, and the second kind of presence matters as
much as the first — the films about his son are substantially about a man who is
not in them.
