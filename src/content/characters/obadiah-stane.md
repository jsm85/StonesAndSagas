---
name: Obadiah Stane
aka:
  - Iron Monger
summary: Stark Industries' second in command, who preferred the company without its founder.
---

Obadiah Stane held Stark Industries together after Howard Stark's death and
never quite accepted that it belonged to someone else. He is the film's argument
about the arms trade made into a person: what the company becomes when nobody in
it has a crisis of conscience.
