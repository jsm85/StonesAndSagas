---
name: Wanda Maximoff
aka:
  - Scarlet Witch
summary: A volunteer test subject whose power reshapes reality, and whose grief eventually reshapes a town.
---

Wanda Maximoff's abilities come from an experiment she agreed to, and every
subsequent loss in her life lands on someone who can, at some scale, refuse to
accept it. That is the engine of her story: the distance between a power that
can rewrite what is real and a person who has been given every reason to want
to.
