---
name: Johann Schmidt
aka:
  - Red Skull
summary: HYDRA's founder, an early recipient of the same serum, and a believer in power without limit.
---

Johann Schmidt took an unfinished version of the same treatment that made Steve
Rogers, and it gave him what he already was rather than what he hoped for. He
runs HYDRA as a science division that outgrows the regime it serves, chasing
artefacts he understands as tools of gods and intends to use as ordnance.
