---
name: Jasper Sitwell
summary: A S.H.I.E.L.D. agent whose job is the paperwork after the disaster.
---

Sitwell handles the unglamorous end of S.H.I.E.L.D.: recovery, containment and
the awkward conversations with civilians who have picked something up they
should not have. He is the organisation seen from the middle of it rather than
the top.
