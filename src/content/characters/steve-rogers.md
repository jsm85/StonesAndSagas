---
name: Steve Rogers
aka:
  - Captain America
summary: A frail volunteer made into a soldier, whose defining quality was never the strength.
---

Steve Rogers is chosen for an experimental programme precisely because he has
spent his life being overmatched and has never once let that decide his
behaviour. The serum makes him strong; it changes nothing else about him. What
follows is a career of being the person in the room who will not move when
moving would be easier.
