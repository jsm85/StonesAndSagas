name: Agatha Harkness
aka:
  - Agnes
summary: A witch of long standing, introduced as the nosy neighbour next door.
---

Agatha Harkness arrives as a sitcom fixture — the neighbour who lets herself in
— and holds that shape for as long as it is useful to her. She is old, practised
and interested in power as a subject of study, which makes her curiosity about
Wanda Maximoff considerably less friendly than it first appears.
