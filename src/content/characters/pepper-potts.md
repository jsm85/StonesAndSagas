---
name: Pepper Potts
summary: Stark's chief of staff and eventual chief executive, the one person who reliably tells him no.
---

Pepper Potts runs the parts of Tony Stark's life he cannot be trusted with,
which turns out to include most of them. She begins as his assistant and ends up
running the company, and across that arc she is consistently the character with
the clearest read on what Stark is actually doing and why.
