---
name: Tony Stark
aka:
  - Iron Man
summary: An arms manufacturer turned armoured hero, forced into a conscience by the weapons he sold.
---

Heir to a weapons company and a genuinely brilliant engineer, Tony Stark spends
the first part of his life insulated from what his products do. Held captive
with shrapnel working its way toward his heart, he builds a suit to get himself
out, and then keeps building — first to unmake the arms trade he grew rich on,
later because he cannot stop trying to armour the world against the next threat.
His flaw and his method are the same one: he would rather engineer a solution
alone than trust anyone else with the problem.
